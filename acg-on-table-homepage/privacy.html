Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026
Last updated: September 29, 2026

The ACG On Table development team ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use ACG On Table and its related account services.

We never ask for your Google, Facebook, or Apple password, and we do not sell personal data received through third-party sign-in services.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the ACG On Table visionOS application, its account and sign-in services, and webpages that link to this policy. Third-party websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information We May Collect

Third-Party Account Information

When you choose to sign in with a third-party account, we may receive the following information with your authorization:

  • Google: account identifier, name, email address, email verification status, and profile picture;
  • Facebook: account identifier, public profile information, name, email address, and profile picture;
  • Apple: account identifier, the name you choose to share, and either your email address or an Apple private relay address.

Service and Security Information

To secure sign-in, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the service, our servers may record request times, IP addresses, browser or device type, operating system version, error logs, and information about relevant security events.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, identify, and maintain your ACG On Table account;
  • To authenticate sign-in and display profile information you authorize;
  • To sync collections and app settings associated with your account;
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • To diagnose problems, improve reliability, and provide support;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose limited information to infrastructure or technical service providers that process it on our behalf under appropriate contractual and confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information with your consent, to protect legal rights and safety, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, although no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may revoke ACG On Table's access through the authorization settings of the relevant third-party account. Revoking third-party access does not automatically delete an ACG On Table account that has already been created.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated personal data, use the account settings in the app or email cnhjr230329@outlook.com. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable period, subject to data we must retain by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children below the applicable age of digital consent in their country or region. If we learn that we collected a child's personal information without appropriate parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

8. International Processing

Depending on your location and the infrastructure used to provide the service, information may be processed outside your country or region. Where required, we use reasonable safeguards for international processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the product, technology, or applicable law. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Where appropriate, we will provide additional notice of material changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or our handling of personal data, contact the ACG On Table development team at cnhjr230329@outlook.com.
